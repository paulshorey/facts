# Research rotation

Give each round a fixed number of questions. For a six-question round, assign
four slots to distinct subjects, one to replication or a conflicting source, and
one to an existing investigation. Treat that allocation as a starting experiment.
Move a question into the depth slot when a unit, denominator, fiscal period or
source discrepancy could materially change what a reader takes away.

## Choosing and closing a question

1. Run `.venv/bin/python -m facts.portfolio`. Inspect the least represented
   subjects and the originating URLs behind them. Give an unserved category a
   turn before giving a second slot to one already in the round. Alternate
   national and local questions, who benefits and who bears costs, and measures
   of improvement and deterioration. Apply the same evidentiary standard to all.
2. Choose a dated table, registry or original survey. Save its bytes, publication
   and observation dates, geography, population, denominator, units, revision,
   and exact row or page. Review at least one plausible alternate interpretation.
   Count releases or independent data producers alongside item counts. Separate
   a second host of a PDF from independent corroboration.
3. Search for the particular statistic and for paraphrases, using the same
   geography and period. Predeclare the outlet panel and time window. Include
   national outlets with different editorial traditions, a wire service, and
   local or specialist sources. Record the query, retrieval date, access gaps,
   candidate links, and whether coverage was an exact match, a related story or
   a syndicated copy. An unavailable archive is unknown. A search without a
   match supports only a bounded report about that search.
4. Export a short observation with source and method. Mark genuinely contested
   interpretations and unresolved checks. After the round, compare distinct
   categories and publishers, checked observations per staff hour, corrections,
   and prior reporting discovered. Retire a lead once its question is answered.
   Promote at most one follow-up into the next depth slot.

water supply. Each has a native topic definition and one discovery search for
a future refresh. The retained pilot and coverage receipts are in
